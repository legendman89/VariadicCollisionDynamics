============================================================
KEYBOARD SCANCODES (DirectInput DIK Codes)
============================================================

NOTE:
These are raw scancodes (0–255, extended codes exceed 255).
Decimal and Hex are interchangeable.
Use these values in CustomDodge.ini (e.g., DodgeKey = 47 or 0x2F).
